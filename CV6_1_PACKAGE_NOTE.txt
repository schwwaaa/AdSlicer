AdSlicer CV-6.1 — Raised-Black Recovery

Purpose
-------
Recover a real VHS/TV structural boundary that CV-6 missed because its fade floor
sat just above the nominal dark threshold while remaining spatially almost flat.

Observed real regression
------------------------
The first CV-6 output segment was ~18.385 s and merged two intended top-level
clips. Comparison against the 99.232 s source found the missing boundary around
3.14 s.

At the valley the source is approximately:
- mean luma: 32.35
- luma stddev: 3.4
- max near-black ratio: ~0.45

This is too bright for the old mean-luma <= 32 rule and not pixel-dark enough
for near_black_ratio >= 0.90, but it is far more spatially uniform than normal
program imagery. Visually it is the raised-gray floor of a VHS fade between two
complete pieces.

Change
------
CV-2 temporal dark evidence now also accepts a conservative raised-uniform rule:
- mean luma <= 40
- luma stddev <= 6

This does NOT globally raise the black threshold. Structured dark title cards,
faces, and graphics remain rejected by this extra criterion because their luma
variance is much higher.

CV-6 still decides structural vs internal role separately, so a uniform internal
fade can remain inside Complete Segments while Every Separator can expose it.

Regression
----------
Synthetic case 10 adds a gray-36 separator that has 0% pixels <= 32 and therefore
reproduces the old miss. Complete Segments is expected to recover the separator,
preserve full timeline coverage, and keep the neighboring 15-second block whole.

Testing contract
----------------
Same command as before:
  ./test-opencv.sh --synthetic
  ./test-opencv.sh --input "/path/to/video.mp4"

Production AdSlicer detector/planner/renderer/frontend remain untouched.
